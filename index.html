FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Quick answers before you order your RAWQ T-shirt.

What is the price of a T-shirt?+

The listed price is ₹599 per full T-shirt. Please confirm availability and delivery charges before ordering.

What sizes are available?+

The order form offers S, M, L, XL and XXL. Please confirm your preferred size is available.

How can I place an order?+

Choose a collection and design, select your size and quantity, and use the WhatsApp order button to send your enquiry.

How can I pay?+

Contact RAWQ to confirm the available payment methods before making a payment.

How long does delivery take?+

Delivery time depends on your location and product availability. Please confirm the estimated delivery date and shipping charges before ordering.

Can I request a custom design?+

Yes. Use the Customize section to enquire about printing your own artwork, name, quote or concept.

How can I contact RAWQ?+

Email rawqwears@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.
